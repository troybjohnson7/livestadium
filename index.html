Live Stadium Flasher

Put the Live Stadium scoreboard software on your 7" screen. About 3 minutes. After this, updates arrive by themselves over WiFi.

You need

  1. The screen: Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-7 (800 x 480, touch). The 1024 x 600 "Type B" will not work.
  2. A USB-C data cable. Charge-only cables don't work: if nothing shows up in step 3, try another cable.
  3. A computer with Chrome or Edge (not Safari or Firefox, not a phone).

Install

  1. Plug the cable into the screen's port marked USB (not "UART"), then into your computer.
  2. Click Install Live Stadium below.
  3. Pick the port that appears (it says "USB JTAG/serial" or "ESP32-S3"), then Connect.
  4. Choose Install. Say yes to erase on a new screen.
  5. Wait for "Installation complete" (1–2 minutes). Unplug, and plug the stadium into power.
This browser can't flash. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page from https:// (not a saved file).

No port shows up?

  1. Unplug the screen.
  2. Hold the BOOT button on the back of the screen while you plug it back in, then let go.
  3. Click Install again. When it's done, unplug and plug back in.

Then set it up from your phone

  1. The screen shows SETUP and a WiFi name like LiveStadium-1A2B3C.
  2. Join that WiFi on your phone. A setup page opens (or go to 192.168.4.1).
  3. Pick your home WiFi (2.4 GHz), team, time zone and stadium. Tap Save.

Done. Long-press the screen any time for Settings.
